About Story Cast

Explore Sudanese culture, music, and landscapes through audio, video, and photography.

Our mission

Story Cast celebrates Sudan's cultural diversity and helps visitors discover its stories and creative voices.

Through recordings, musical performances, and photographs, the site connects visitors with Sudanese traditions, everyday experiences, and landscapes.

Using this site

Visit the Home page to explore Sudanese culture, listen to the featured recording, and view landscape photography.

You can also read the recording transcript .

Visit the Music page to explore featured performances and learn about the artists. Select “More” to expand each performance description.

Accessibility

Story Cast includes features intended to make its content easier to navigate and access.

  • A text transcript accompanies the culture recording.
  • Images include alternative text.
  • Headings organize the content into sections.
  • Links have visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Layouts adapt to the available screen width.
  • Expandable descriptions can be opened and closed using the keyboard.

Keyboard navigation

Press Tab to move between links and controls, and Shift + Tab to move backward. Press Enter to follow a link. When a “More” control is focused, press Enter or Space to expand or collapse it.

Video captions

Videos are embedded from YouTube. When captions are available, use the player's CC control to turn them on. Caption availability and accuracy depend on the individual video.
